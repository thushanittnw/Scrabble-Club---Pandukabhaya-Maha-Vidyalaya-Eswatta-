What You Can Learn

Scrabble is used here as a fun way to encourage English language learning. The learning vocabulary focuses on useful English for Sri Lankan school students.

📖 English Vocabulary

Our vocabulary focuses on useful English for students. Words are taught with simple A1-level explanations.

Our dictionary principle: This learning system does not depend on a commercial Scrabble dictionary. Vocabulary is selected and curated for English learning. Teachers may add words using legally permitted references and their own teaching materials.
