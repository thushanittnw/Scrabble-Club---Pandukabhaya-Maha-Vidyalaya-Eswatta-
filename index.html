What You Can Learn

📚 Scrabble Rules

Learn the board, tiles, word placement, turns, exchanges, challenges and game ending.

🔤 Competitive Words

Learn useful short words, hooks, extensions, anagrams and word patterns.

⭐ Scoring

Learn letter values, premium squares and how to calculate scores.

🧠 Word Practice

Practise anagrams, hooks, extensions and difficult letters.

🎯 Play & Learn

Practise on an interactive Scrabble board while learning from your moves.

🏆 Improve Your Game

Develop vocabulary, scoring skills, rack management and strategy.

Your Learning Journey

1. Learn

2. Practise

Try word and scoring activities.

3. Play

Use what you learned in practice games.

4. Improve

Build competitive vocabulary and strategy.
